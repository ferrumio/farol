## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play — technical, product, team?
State the problem neutrally, before the decision. Include facts and constraints,
not the conclusion.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? State it in the active voice: "We will ...". Be
specific enough that someone can act on it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder, as a result? Include follow-up
work this decision creates and risks it leaves open. Cover both the good and
the bad — an ADR with only upsides is hiding something.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why was it not chosen?
